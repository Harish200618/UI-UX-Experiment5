# UI-UX-Experiment5

## Aim:
Develop an effective digital solution (e.g., an app or platform) that helps students:

Manage academic stress to prevent burnout and increase productivity.

Track their moods and habits to improve emotional self-awareness and identify stress triggers.

## Algorithm:
Phase 1: Data Gathering (Input)	Surveys	Collect quantitative data on student stress levels, coping mechanisms, and usage of existing tools. Algorithm: Statistical analysis of survey responses (e.g., mean stress scores, correlation between habits and stress).
Interviews	Gather qualitative data on emotional challenges, personal experiences, and needs. Algorithm: Thematic analysis or grounded theory to identify key pain points and needs from student narratives.

Phase 2: Design & Prototyping	(Implied)	Design a solution based on the data, incorporating features that address the pain points of personas like Aisha and Rahul (e.g., routines, tracking, meditation integration).

Phase 3: Validation (Testing)	Usability Testing	Evaluate the user-friendliness and intuitiveness of the relaxation and tracking features. Algorithm: Task success rates, time-on-task, and System Usability Scale (SUS) to measure ease of navigation and feature effectiveness.
